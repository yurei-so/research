# Meta Research

Meta Research studies the apparatus, representations, and workflows used to
produce, preserve, evaluate, discover, and communicate Yūrei research.

Ordinary site maintenance is not Meta Research. Work belongs here only when it
states a research question, freezes a protocol, records observations, and
supports an interpretable result.

## Completed experiment

- `meta-research-001` — grounded build-time labnote summarization. The private
  shadow campaign found that short academic prose was not useful, then passed
  8/8 candidates after adopting the conversational Crayon Speak contract. No
  generated orientation is public merely because the experiment succeeded.
